# CAMPAIGN POSITIONING — H.C.C.

## Product

H.C.C — Hunting Cyber Criminals. A fictional spy/hacking simulation played as a Telegram Mini App and on web. The player runs operations against invented cyber-criminal targets, builds a rig, mines, and climbs an operator rank ladder.

## Positioning statements

**Primary**
> You start as nobody. You end the night knowing who they really are.

**Secondary**
> Build the operation. Run the hunt. Own the night.

**Call to action**
> PLAY H.C.C FREE

**Mandatory legal/disclaimer line on all creative**
> Fiction. Targets, operators and payloads are invented.

## Core promise

Earned mastery. The player begins with one desk and nothing to their name, and ends holding a closed case and a name. Every capability is earned, not given.

## Creative pillars

1. **The Reveal** — the payoff of a completed hunt. Identity confirmed, dossier closed.
2. **The Climb** — visible progression: Script Kiddie → Operator → Ghost → Architect → Phantom.
3. **The Open Case** — unfinished business. A live target, 2/4 operations logged, trace ongoing.

## Tone

Warm, quiet, literary, brief, honest. Never hype-driven. No emojis, no exclamation marks, no fake urgency, no claims of real hacking capability. All targets, handles, hosts and payloads shown in creative are fictional.

## Audience

- Idle/simulation and management-game players
- Cyberpunk, hacker-fiction and Matrix-adjacent aesthetic audiences
- Telegram Mini App and crypto-adjacent game communities

## Funnel roles

| Stage | Creative | Message job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cold / top | Poster 1 — The Reveal, Video "Intercept." | Establish the fantasy and payoff in under 3 seconds |
| Consideration | Poster 2 — The Climb | Show depth and long-term progression |
| Retargeting | Poster 3 — The Open Case | Reopen the loop for people who bounced |
